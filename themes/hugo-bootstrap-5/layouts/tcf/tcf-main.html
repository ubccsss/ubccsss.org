Welcome to TCF 2027!

The UBC Computer Science Technical Career Fair (TCF) is a student-led event that connects employers with technical talent at one of the world’s top public universities. Held in collaboration with campus tech groups that champion women and LGBTQ+ students, this year’s TCF spotlights both technical excellence and diverse talent.

For companies, the TCF is a direct line to the next generation of engineers and technologists coming out of UBC. This in-person event gives your company the chance to meet 4500+ students across Computer Science, Cognitive Systems, Data Science, Statistics, Computer Engineering, and Electrical Engineering. It’s an opportunity to recruit motivated, technically-skilled talent, strengthen your reputation as an inclusive employer of choice, and build a lasting presence at one of Canada’s leading CS departments.
