      Hi everyone!

      We are seeking graduate students, senior undergrads, postdocs, and faculty to review
      submissions for the 2nd edition of the UBC Computer Science Student Research Conference

      1-page extended abstracts + posters.

      We will have a ~2-week review period. This year, we are implementing a voting mechanism

      for their preferred submissions to review.

      - **Voting period:** September 19th&ndash;24th
      - **Reviewing period:** September 26th&ndash;October 8th
      - **Tracks:** short papers or posters
      - **Time commitment:** ~2 hours total
      - **Duties:** evaluate submissions using a provided rubric for clarity, originality,
        methods, and impact; provide concise constructive comments; declare conflicts
      - **Benefits:** acknowledgement in the program and a participation certificate on
        request

  - subject: Call for Submissions
    date: August 17, 2026
    message: |
      Hi everyone!

      We are super excited to announce the call for submissions for the 2nd edition of the
      UBC Computer Science Student Research Conference (CSSRC), happening **Friday, November
      6th** in the Atrium.

      We invite all students to submit their work, including ongoing, early-stage,
      course-based, or exploratory research. If you know anyone in the department who may be
      interested in participating, please encourage them to submit!

      **Submission Portal:** [https://hotcrp-cssrc.cs.ubc.ca/](https://hotcrp-cssrc.cs.ubc.ca/)\*

      \* To ensure security, the portal is only accessible within the UBC Network. If you are
      on campus, connect to `ubcsecure`. Otherwise, connect to the UBC CS VPN &mdash; guide
      available [here](https://my.cs.ubc.ca/docs/connecting-vpn).

      **Submission Tracks**

      - **Short Paper + Oral Presentation:** 4-page paper following the
        [ACM template](https://www.acm.org/publications/authors/submissions) &mdash; to be
        included in the archival CSSRC 2026.2 Booklet (CSGSA website).
      - **Poster + Poster Presentation:** 1-page abstract + 36"x48" poster (vertical/portrait
        orientation) &mdash;
        [example template available](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/downloads/print-and-presentation-templates/).

      **Timeline**

      - **September 18:** Registration Deadline (title + abstract submission, max 250 words)
      - **September 25:** Full Paper or Poster Submission Deadline
      - **October 9:** Notifications sent out
      - **October 23:** Camera-Ready Deadline (for accepted works)
      - **November 6:** Conference Day!

      **Concurrent Submissions and Archival Concerns**

      We want this conference to be inclusive of ongoing work. If you are concerned about
      double-blind requirements or exclusivity rules of other conferences (such as ICLR), we
      can handle these on a case-by-case basis (e.g. opting out of public PDF distribution or
      archiving). Please do not let this deter you from submitting!

      **Questions?** We have set up a Discord server for real-time Q&A regarding submissions
      and the conference schedule. [Join the CSSRC Discord here](https://discord.gg/uWPuBTYwhs).

      Stay tuned for the full conference schedule, registration link, and calls for reviewers
      and referees! We very much look forward to your submissions &mdash; please don't
      hesitate to contact us if any issues or questions arise.

      Best regards,

      Vivian White, CSGSA VP Academics

      *on behalf of the CSSRC Organizing Committee (CSGSA & CSSS)*
